A vocal coach trained in the Agra Gharana lineage of Hindustani classical music.

I am a vocal coach and music teacher trained in North Indian (Hindustani) classical music under the lineage of the Agra Gharana. I trained for more than 10 years and have been teaching since 2020. I am also a certified Reiki healer, a Vipassana practitioner and a former dentist.

The sessions I've designed draw on Shastriya Sangeet, Dhyana and my ongoing study of mantras. Blending all three increases your concentration and awareness, so you capture and remember lessons with ease and practise better on your own.

Session structure

How a session unfolds

Each session runs for between 90 minutes and 2 hours.

  1. Meditation

    Settle in and arrive.

    5–9 min

  2. Beeja mantra chant

    Seed syllables to activate the chakras.

  3. Invocation

    An invocation or prayer opens the main session.

  4. Vocal technique and raaga

    Voice work, with raaga practice once beginner level is complete.

  5. Closing mantra

    A simple mantra, or a more complex one if you're an enthusiast, with an invocation.

  6. Silence

    One minute of stillness to close.

    1 min
